Questions

Good to know

Is it really free?

Yes. Your first 20 blog posts are included, with no credit card. After that, one credit is one finished post — you're only charged when a post is ready.

Which computers does it run on?

Mac (Apple Silicon and Intel, signed and notarized by Apple) and Windows. One download, nothing else to install.

Will the posts sound like my business?

Zippy reads your website first, then asks you about anything it isn't sure of — your button, your prices, who each product is for. Articles only say what's true about your product.

Where do the posts get published?

Straight to WordPress or a Lovable site, through a webhook, or a connection Zippy builds for your platform. Or download them and paste them in yourself.

How do I install it on a Mac?

Open the .dmg and drag Zippy Blog into Applications, then open it from there. It updates itself after that.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC"?

That's SmartScreen being careful with a new app. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
